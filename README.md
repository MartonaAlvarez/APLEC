# APLEC (Avaluació de PLatihelmints i artròpodes microEndèmics dels països Catalans)
Information regarding the sequencing project of microendemisms from Catalonia. Funded by IEC and CBP.

The goal of APLEC is to obtain chromosome-level genomes for three microendemic invertebrate species from the Catalan Countries: the freshwater planarian *Dugesia corbata* (Mallorca), the terrestrial planarian *Microplana plurioculata*, and the springtail *Lepidocyrtus montseniensis* (found in Montseny and nearby mountains), the latter of which shares a habitat with *M. plurioculata* and may serve as its prey. These taxa are microendemics associated with unique microhabitats and represent poorly explored evolutionary lineages.
The project addresses a significant gap in genomic resources. Despite their relevance to studies on regeneration, genome evolution, and adaptation, planarians—especially terrestrial ones—are underrepresented in genome databases. Currently, there is no available genome for any European terrestrial planarian, nor are there any reference genomes for planarians or springtails endemic to the Catalan region. *D. corbata* is one of the few exclusively sexual freshwater flatworms, while *M. plurioculata* exhibits distinctive morphological traits that are rare within its genus.
Generating high-quality genomes for these organisms is technically challenging due to their small size, the difficulty of obtaining high-molecular-weight DNA, and the fact that their genomes are highly repetitive and AT-rich. Beyond generating unique genomic resources for endemic fauna, this project will establish transferable methodological frameworks that will support future research into the evolution, adaptation, and conservation of microendemic species.
